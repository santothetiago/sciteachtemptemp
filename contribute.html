Submission Standards

Clear Legibility: PDF scans or digital documents must be crisp, with legible mathematical symbols and labeled diagrams.
Curriculum Alignment: Content must directly correspond to DepEd / USTP Senior High School STEM competencies.
Answer Keys Included: Practice sets and reviewers should include worked solutions or comprehensive answer keys.
Cloud Sharing: Ensure Google Drive permissions are configured to "Viewer" for all students.

Contribute via GitHub PR

For students familiar with Git: fork the repository, append your resource object into assets/js/data.js, and open a Pull Request!
